MISSION BRIEFING

TOP SECRET

◈OBJECTIVE & MODES

Two submarines hunt each other across a 30×20 grid. Each boat has 3 HP. BLUE deploys in the bottom three rows, RED in the top three. Direct hits deal 2 damage, indirect (adjacent blast) deals 1; explosions chain through nearby torpedoes and mines.

SOLO PATROL — you command BLUE against the computer. HOT-SEAT DUEL — two humans share one screen as BLUE and RED; an opaque hand-off screen hides the chart between turns so neither player sees the other's private picture. Before every match, each commander picks their own starting tile inside their deployment zone.

◈THE TURN

Each turn: optionally move 0–4 spaces in a straight line, and optionally take one action (launch, mine, sonar, or reload). Actions resolve from your start-of-turn position, even if a move is plotted. Click a highlighted cell to plot; click it again to undo. Press END TURN to commit — your boat then moves instantly.

Actions must be armed (button selected), positioned on the board, then confirmed with LAUNCH. Clicking an armed button again disarms it.

◈NOISE & ESTIMATOR BOXES

Everything you do makes noise. After your turn, a hollow box in your color shows where the enemy thinks you are — sized by your noise. The opponent's box is your estimate of them. It always contains the true boat.

◈TORPEDOES

Tubes 1–3 start loaded with torpedoes; tube 4 with a decoy. Arm a tube, pick one of the 4 adjacent cells, LAUNCH. The torpedo is wire-guided: guide it exactly 5 cells per turn, one click at a time, never reversing onto its last cell. You must guide all active torpedoes before ending the turn.

Each torpedo runs for 4 turns, then detonates — blasting all 8 adjacent cells. Moving onto the enemy = direct hit (2 dmg). The launching tube stays in WIRE state until the run ends. Selecting a run shades its maximum reach envelope. Opposing torpedoes appear only after their first invisible 5-cell run.

◈DECOYS & MINES

DECOYS launch 4 cells out in a cardinal direction, then drift 4 cells/turn in a straight line. Each decoy emits its own fake contact box in your color — the enemy sees two of you. Decoys die at the board edge, on contact with a sub, or in any blast. 4 aboard.

MINES deploy to any of the 8 adjacent cells and are invisible to the enemy. An enemy sub crossing one takes a direct 2-damage hit. Torpedoes pass over mines harmlessly. 5 aboard. At 0, the mine button is dead for the match.

◈SONAR & RELOAD

SONAR sweeps a radius-8 box around you. If the enemy is inside, it is revealed until the end of your next turn — and its estimator box vanishes from your picture. But a ping is +8 noise: you reveal yourself too. If the enemy pings you, your own box collapses to your exact position. Evade!

RELOAD (the turn's action) cycles the selected tube: loaded tubes toggle torpedo↔decoy; empty tubes cycle torpedo→decoy→empty. Cycling back to the original load cancels the action. Wire tubes cannot reload. Decoy reloads draw from reserves.

◈TACTICAL PLOT

Press P or the PLOT MODE button to annotate the chart. Click any cell to drop a private mark — CONTACT, DANGER, or NAV — click a mark to remove it. Marks cost no action and make no noise, and are kept separately for each commander. CONTACT HISTORY keeps the last few enemy estimate boxes as dashed ghosts so you can read their drift. Toggle it with H.

◈CONTROLS

1–4 tubes · R reload · M mine · S sonar · L launch · ENTER end turn · P plot mode · H history · ESC / right-click cancel. Hover any cell for its grid reference. Pulsating torpedoes demand guidance — the white numbers count down cells and turns. Solo service record and ribbons persist between patrols.
